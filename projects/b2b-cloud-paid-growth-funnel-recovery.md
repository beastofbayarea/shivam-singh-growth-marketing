# B2B Cloud Paid Growth — Funnel Recovery

> **Portfolio lens:** Paid acquisition, rapid funnel diagnosis, creative testing, CAC efficiency, and retention quality.

## Executive snapshot

Recovered a deteriorating B2B paid funnel through controlled isolation rather than a wholesale relaunch. Live instrumentation exposed keyword waste, creative decay, and audience drift; a 30% treatment budget and predefined rollback protected the baseline while the team restored relevance.

## Resume-ready impact

- Diagnosed a 20% CTR decline and 15% CAC increase within 90 minutes using joined ad, web, and CRM data.
- Paused proven waste, moved $50K to exact-match intent, launched ten new ads in 48 hours, and narrowed targeting to actual decision makers.
- Reduced CPA from $65 to $48, increased CTR 25%, generated 200 additional qualified leads, and lowered dead-page waste below 2% in 14 days.

## Interview story

### Situation

A target of 2,500 trials was at risk. Twenty percent of spend sat on keywords below 1% CTR, creative engagement had decayed 30%, and audience targeting had drifted away from buyers.

### Task

Repair current-quarter acquisition economics without damaging future demand or turning off campaigns that continued to perform.

### Actions

- Kept the strongest 60% of the baseline live and tested new creative, intent, and roles on 30% of budget.
- Set a $75 CPA kill switch and used a three-day decision read before scaling.
- Separated a three-week high-intent tourniquet from a subsequent awareness rebuild.
- Maintained unchanged holdout campaigns and evaluated six-month retention, not only immediate CPA.

### Results

- CPA fell from $65 to $48.
- CTR increased 25%.
- The campaign added 200 qualified leads and avoided $75K in waste.
- Six-month retention was 20% higher, and next-quarter baseline efficiency remained roughly 20% stronger.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Do not conflate high-intent recovery with long-term demand creation.
- Scale only after the treatment beat an unchanged control.
- Preserve a minimum share of genuinely new visitors during awareness rebuilding.

## Leadership signal

Used shared live metrics to turn a budget crisis into a fast, reversible testing program across media, creative, web, CRM, and sales stakeholders.
