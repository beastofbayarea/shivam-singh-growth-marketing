## Evidence-grounded premise

The FTC requires advertising claims to be truthful, non-deceptive, and evidence-based. The World Bank's impact-evaluation handbook explains counterfactual reasoning and experimental or quasi-experimental approaches. These sources support diagnosing a B2B cloud funnel through claim quality, buyer intent, and causal tests rather than click volume alone.

## Case approach

- Audit audience, promise, evidence, landing experience, qualification, and sales handoff as one system.
- Remove or qualify unsupported claims and map material promises to verifiable product evidence.
- Segment by buyer problem and test message, offer, and handoff using controlled comparisons.
- Allocate spend according to qualified pipeline and incremental outcomes, not platform attribution alone.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use substantiated-claim coverage, qualified conversion, incremental lift, sales acceptance, cycle progression, acquisition cost, and complaint signals. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
