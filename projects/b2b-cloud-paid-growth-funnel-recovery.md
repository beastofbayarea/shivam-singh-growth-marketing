# B2B Cloud Paid Growth — Funnel Recovery

> **Portfolio lens:** Market-entry growth strategy, performance-funnel auditing, CRO, channel economics, controlled acquisition testing, and retention quality.

> **Primary resume experience:** McKinsey & Company - Business Analyst, Market Entry (2014 - 2016).

## Executive snapshot

Converted a deteriorating B2B paid funnel into a quantified growth-strategy and CRO program. Live instrumentation exposed keyword waste, creative decay, and audience drift; a 30% treatment budget, unchanged controls, retention measurement, and predefined rollback protected the baseline while the team identified a more sustainable channel mix.

## Resume-ready impact

- Audited the acquisition funnel across ad, web, and CRM data, diagnosing a 20% CTR decline and 15% CAC increase within 90 minutes.
- Built a controlled channel-reallocation case, moving $50K to exact-match intent, launching ten new ads in 48 hours, and narrowing targeting to actual decision makers.
- Reduced CPA from $65 to $48, increased CTR 25%, generated 200 additional qualified leads, lowered dead-page waste below 2%, and preserved a 20% six-month retention advantage.

## Interview story

### Situation

A target of 2,500 trials was at risk. Twenty percent of spend sat on keywords below 1% CTR, creative engagement had decayed 30%, and audience targeting had drifted away from buyers.

### Task

Repair current-quarter acquisition economics and create an evidence-based CRO roadmap without damaging future demand or turning off campaigns that continued to perform.

### Actions

- Kept the strongest 60% of the baseline live and tested new creative, intent, and roles on 30% of budget.
- Set a $75 CPA kill switch and used a three-day decision read before scaling.
- Separated a three-week high-intent tourniquet from a subsequent awareness rebuild.
- Joined channel, landing-page, CRM, and retention signals into one performance-funnel view.
- Maintained unchanged holdout campaigns and evaluated six-month retention, not only immediate CPA, so the recommendation distinguished sustainable acquisition from subsidized volume.

### Results

- CPA fell from $65 to $48.
- CTR increased 25%.
- The campaign added 200 qualified leads and avoided $75K in waste.
- Six-month retention was 20% higher, and next-quarter baseline efficiency remained roughly 20% stronger.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Do not conflate high-intent recovery with long-term market development.
- Scale only after the treatment beat an unchanged control.
- Preserve a minimum share of genuinely new visitors during awareness rebuilding.
- Treat channel mix, conversion, and retention as one customer-economics decision rather than three separate reports.

## Leadership signal

Converted an ambiguous funnel decline into an executive-ready growth case and a fast, reversible testing program across media, creative, web, CRM, and sales stakeholders.
