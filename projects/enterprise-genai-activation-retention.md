# Enterprise GenAI Growth — Activation, Retention & Work Saved

> **Portfolio lens:** PLG activation, lifecycle growth, behavioral segmentation, expansion, and value-based monetization.

## Executive snapshot

Shifted an enterprise AI growth system from logins and shallow usage to verified work saved. Zero-configuration onboarding, high-value behavior scoring, contextual nudges, team-level ROI, controlled experimentation, and outcome-led win-back drove deeper adoption and commercial expansion.

## Resume-ready impact

- Redefined the north star from daily activity to verified work saved across roughly 200 benchmarked workflows.
- Combined zero-configuration onboarding, behavioral scoring, contextual next actions, and anonymized team ROI to increase free-to-paid conversion from 2.4% to 8.7%.
- Raised NRR from 102% to 128%, reduced churn from 14% to 6.5%, and shortened trial-to-paid time from 42 days to 18.

## Interview story

### Situation

Traffic looked strong, but only 35% of users connected the context required for deep value. Developers stayed in autocomplete, business users treated the product as search, and buyers could not prove team ROI.

### Task

Move users from curiosity through activation, deep workflow value, monetization, expansion, and retention without relying on generic campaigns or invasive individual surveillance.

### Actions

- Scored advanced completed work above chat frequency and autocomplete volume.
- Auto-detected environments and generated setup, reducing documentation friction.
- Triggered contextual product guidance from current user behavior and task state.
- Stopped individual leaderboards and created anonymized team dashboards showing collective outcomes.

### Results

- Conversion increased 262%, from 2.4% to 8.7%.
- NRR rose 26 points, from 102% to 128%.
- Trial-to-paid time fell 57%, from 42 days to 18.
- Churn declined 53%, from 14% to 6.5%, with 12,400 work-years reported as the value north star.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Measure completed work and ROI rather than logins or generated-text volume.
- Use team-level proof instead of individual productivity surveillance.
- Bound agentic trial cost with explicit token and workflow kill switches.

## Leadership signal

Aligned Data Science, Product, Engineering, Sales, Finance, and enterprise buyers around one observable value ladder and controlled experiment design.
