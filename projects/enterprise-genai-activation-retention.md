## Evidence-grounded premise

The 2020 RAG paper showed how retrieval can provide updateable external knowledge and more factual language than a parametric-only baseline. GOV.UK's user-research guidance requires continuous observation of real users and outcome-focused service design. These contemporaneous sources support activation around a narrow, evidence-backed work outcome rather than broad feature exposure.

## Case approach

- Select a bounded knowledge task with observable user need, source material, and error cost.
- Design first-run experience around completing that task with cited retrieved evidence.
- Observe real workflows, identify abandonment and trust failures, and iterate in small cohorts.
- Define retention as repeated verified value, not login frequency alone.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use task completion, citation coverage, unsupported-claim rate, time to first verified value, repeat task use, and abandonment reasons. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
