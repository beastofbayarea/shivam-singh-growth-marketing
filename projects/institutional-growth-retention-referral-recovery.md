# Institutional Growth - Retention and Referral Recovery

> **Portfolio lens:** Lifecycle growth, behavioral churn signals, customer transparency, retention, referrals, NPS, and AUM expansion.

## Executive snapshot

Led a coordinated product and lifecycle response when a $5B emerging-markets strategy entered a new volatility regime. Behavioral churn signals, proactive customer communication, a transparent risk portal, and evidence-led recovery journeys repaired trust while turning retention into referral-driven growth.

## Resume-ready impact

- Protected customer relationships during a $5B fund recovery by connecting portfolio events, behavioral risk signals, and lifecycle outreach.
- Built a transparent customer experience around regime, risk drivers, portfolio posture, liquidity, and guarded scenario analysis.
- Achieved 99.8% retention, protected $225M of identified at-risk assets, added $200M through referrals, and raised NPS from 78 to 96.

## Interview story

### Situation

A strategy designed for mean reversion failed when inflation and geopolitical shocks created a persistent trend regime. Delayed, black-box reporting left customers unable to understand risk and created silent churn exposure.

### Task

Protect customer relationships while the investment team repaired the strategy, then convert transparent recovery evidence into retention, advocacy, and referral growth.

### Actions

- Combined portfolio, engagement, service, and relationship signals to identify at-risk customers before redemption decisions.
- Segmented outreach by exposure, behavior, relationship value, and information need.
- Built a customer portal showing current regime, risk drivers, specialist posture, liquidity, and guarded scenario analysis.
- Coordinated proactive lifecycle communications across portfolio management, risk, execution, and investor-relations teams.
- Used customer questions and engagement patterns to improve education, recovery messaging, and relationship follow-up.

### Results

- Retention reached 99.8%.
- The program protected $225M of $500M in identified at-risk AUM.
- Referrals added $200M in new AUM.
- NPS increased from 78 to 96.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Treat strategy recovery and customer understanding as one lifecycle problem.
- Use guided transparency without exposing proprietary source code.
- Prioritize at-risk relationships through behavioral evidence rather than generic broadcast communication.

## Leadership signal

Aligned portfolio management, quant research, risk, execution, investor relations, and customer-facing teams around one retention model, communication cadence, and evidence-based recovery journey.
