## Evidence-grounded premise

FINRA Rule 2210 requires fair, balanced, non-misleading communications suited to their audience. COSO's internal-control framework emphasizes reliable information, ownership, and monitoring. These sources support institutional retention built on transparent evidence, governed communications, and resolved service issues rather than promotional pressure.

## Case approach

- Segment attrition and referral barriers by mandate fit, evidence needs, service failures, and trust.
- Review institutional communications for balanced risk, material qualifications, and substantiation.
- Create owned feedback, escalation, resolution, and follow-up loops across commercial and operating teams.
- Ask for referrals only after demonstrated service recovery and documented client value.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use issue resolution, communication exceptions, retention by reason, qualified referrals, service recurrence, and evidence completeness. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
