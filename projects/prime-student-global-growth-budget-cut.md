# Engineering a portable Prime Student campaign under a budget cut

The program had half the prior-year budget, a stated ten-market remit, 40% overlap among internal audiences, and an estimated $2 million cost for fully bespoke local production. Then a youth-ad policy change throttled 65% of direct-response assets.

During my AWS tenure, I designed the cross-Amazon operating system spanning students, Prime, Video, Music, local marketers, creators, Media, Privacy/Legal, Analytics, Engineering, Finance, and leaders. Its strategic job was portability when money or channel access disappeared.

## Three forms of portability

### Audience

Participating products could suppress an already reached or converted student without broadly sharing direct identifiers. The source describes salted one-way hashes; stable hashed identifiers remain pseudonymous and linkable.

The full contract required purpose limitation, approved participants, access control, privacy assessment, 30-day retention/deletion, and audit. NIST guidance on [pairwise pseudonymous identifiers](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-3/sp800-63c.html) informed the correlation-risk logic.

Audience overlap fell from 40% to 8%—32 points and 80% relative. On a simplistic uniform $1 million spend base, that implies $320,000 net duplicate capacity removed, not the source’s $400,000. The larger figure may refer to original overlap; auction-level evidence is required.

### Creative

I separated environment, member benefit, campaign hook, format, claim/disclosure, and local adaptation. Global owners controlled proposition and evidence. Local teams controlled credible setting, language, offer expression, and channel. Japan used commuter rather than default US dorm imagery.

Every recombination retained eligibility, youth-policy and claim review, creator disclosure, version, and market approval. AI-assisted content remained draft.

Adaptation moved from 72 hours to six—66 hours and 91.7% faster. Cost claims conflict: 80% lower on a $2 million base implies $400,000 final / $1.6 million saved, while the narrative reports $1.2 million saved. I preserve the conflict instead of choosing the larger benefit.

### Channel

The proposition could move across paid media, creators, email, and owned placements without rebuilding the campaign.

When 65% of direct-response assets were throttled, engagement fell 30% in 48 hours. I activated compliant creator stories and moved eligible remarketing to owned channels. Ten creators received narrative guardrails rather than hard-sell scripts; replacements shipped within 48 hours.

The FTC’s 2023 [guidance on blurred advertising for children and teens](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/09/blurred-ads-kids-teens-what-know) supports visible disclosure and separation. Each market still required local review. The evidence supports approved replacements with no retained violation—not “100% compliance.”

## Markets did different jobs

Budget was allocated 50% drivers, 30% scalers, and 20% incubators.

Drivers had to return contribution and ARPU. Scalers produced subscriber volume. Incubators bought localization or channel learning cheaply. Market cells were measured on incremental starts, trial-to-paid, retention, contribution margin, and marginal ROAS.

The source names only eight countries—Japan, UK, Germany, India, Brazil, Canada, France, Mexico—despite a ten-market claim. I do not invent the missing two.

## Commercial reconciliation

- **Budget:** implied $2M prior year → $1M → 50% lower if scopes match.
- **Overlap:** 40% → 8% → -32 points / -80% relative.
- **Adaptation:** 72 h → 6 h → -91.7%.
- **Policy recovery:** 65% of direct-response assets throttled → replacement set live in 48 h; final reach/incrementality absent.
- **ROAS:** 2.5× → 3.6× → +1.1× / +44%; attribution/spend perimeter absent.
- **US revenue:** $50M target → $48M → 96% attainment, $2M short; not necessarily incremental or campaign-attributed.
- **Global subscribers:** baseline absent → 3.2M program-reported; paid/organic mix and incremental holdout absent.

The $48 million, 3.2 million, 3.6× ROAS, and $1 million budget cannot share one denominator: $48M over $1M would be 48×. They describe different regions, products, attribution scopes, or spend pools and must remain separate.

The retained page combines Prime Student and Rakuten Sports sources, Amazon internal audiences, ten-market structure, and current AWS employment. I present it as a composite cross-Amazon assignment, not an AWS product launch or one fully reconciled interview claim.

Portability, audience rules, the creative component system, youth-policy pivot, market portfolio, measurement, and cadence were the decisions attached to my role. Businesses retained offers/audiences; local teams culture/legal review; Privacy matching; Finance economics.

The durable contribution was resilience engineered before failure: the growth system could substitute audience, creative, and channel components quickly—but only claims whose economics reconciled were allowed to survive.
