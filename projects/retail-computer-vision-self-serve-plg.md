# Retail Computer Vision — Self-Serve Product-Led Growth

> **Portfolio lens:** Product-led growth, product-qualified usage, self-service onboarding, CAC reduction, and conversion.

## Executive snapshot

Replaced a twelve-week, solutions-architect-led trial with a self-service product experience. Edge processing, infrastructure-as-code, pre-flight diagnostics, product-qualified usage, and consumption-aligned sales incentives let customers reach a retail insight before making an enterprise commitment.

## Resume-ready impact

- Removed a 12-week white-glove trial wall by launching a self-service starter kit, edge processing, infrastructure-as-code, and automated environment diagnostics.
- Reduced customer evaluation cycle 75% and CAC 40% by reserving specialists for post-value enterprise scaling.
- Increased paid conversion 15% and redefined qualification around deployed product usage rather than content downloads or demo requests.

## Interview story

### Situation

Retailers wanted computer-vision insights from existing cameras, but every evaluation required custom networking, identity, and security work. Sales effort arrived before the product had demonstrated value.

### Task

Create a low-friction product-led path that preserved privacy and enterprise expansion while removing routine human setup.

### Actions

- Eliminated the managed-service entry point and fixed the trial architecture around one camera and one first insight.
- Processed video at the edge and sent lightweight metadata to reduce bandwidth, privacy, and security friction.
- Built a read-only pre-flight audit for quotas, security groups, IAM, and network paths with clear remediation.
- Defined a product-qualified lead as an account that deployed, processed footage, and viewed an insight; aligned sales reward with consumption.

### Results

- Evaluation cycle fell about 75%, from 12 weeks to roughly three.
- CAC declined 40%.
- Paid conversion increased 15%.
- Specialist capacity shifted from routine trials to higher-value enterprise integrations.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Reserve expert services for expansion after value is proven.
- Defer live video streaming to protect privacy and trial simplicity.
- Reward real consumption rather than theoretical contract size.

## Leadership signal

Aligned product, platform, sales, solutions architecture, security, and customer IT teams around an evidence-led funnel and a reproducible deployment promise.
