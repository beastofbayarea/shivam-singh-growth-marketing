# Retail Computer Vision — Self-Serve Product-Led Growth

## What I worked on

I completed this work during my [AWS experience from 2024 to present](https://github.com/beastofbayarea/shivam-singh-growth-marketing/blob/main/shivam-singh-growth-marketing.pdf).

I replaced a twelve-week, solutions-architect-led trial with a self-service product experience. Edge processing, infrastructure-as-code, pre-flight diagnostics, product-qualified usage, and consumption-aligned sales incentives let customers reach a retail insight before making an enterprise commitment.

## At a glance

- I removed a 12-week white-glove trial wall by launching a self-service starter kit, edge processing, infrastructure-as-code, and automated environment diagnostics.
- I reduced customer evaluation cycle 75% and CAC 40% by reserving specialists for post-value enterprise scaling.
- I increased paid conversion 15% and redefined qualification around deployed product usage rather than content downloads or demo requests.

## The situation

Retailers wanted computer-vision insights from existing cameras, but every evaluation required custom networking, identity, and security work. Sales effort arrived before the product had demonstrated value.

## What I needed to accomplish

I needed to create a low-friction product-led path that preserved privacy and enterprise expansion while removing routine human setup.

## What I did

- I eliminated the managed-service entry point and fixed the trial architecture around one camera and one first insight.
- I processed video at the edge and sent lightweight metadata to reduce bandwidth, privacy, and security friction.
- I built a read-only pre-flight audit for quotas, security groups, IAM, and network paths with clear remediation.
- I defined a product-qualified lead as an account that deployed, processed footage, and viewed an insight; aligned sales reward with consumption.

## The results

- Evaluation cycle fell about 75%, from 12 weeks to roughly three.
- CAC declined 40%.
- Paid conversion increased 15%.
- Specialist capacity shifted from routine trials to higher-value enterprise integrations.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- I reserved expert services for expansion after value is proven.
- I deferred live video streaming to protect privacy and trial simplicity.
- I rewarded real consumption rather than theoretical contract size.

## How I led

I aligned product, platform, sales, solutions architecture, security, and customer IT teams around an evidence-led funnel and a reproducible deployment promise.

## Why I chose this approach

I used [NIST - AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1) to ground aI governance and lifecycle-risk framework.
