## Evidence-grounded premise

NIST's AI RMF requires AI risks to be governed, mapped, measured, and managed. The NIST Privacy Framework addresses privacy risks arising from products that process personal data. Together they support self-serve computer vision only where data purpose, user consent, model limits, monitoring, and correction are understandable without expert intervention.

## Case approach

- Start with a narrow retail task and document intended use, non-use, data, and affected people.
- Make setup validate image quality, permissions, privacy, and minimum performance before activation.
- Expose confidence, failure examples, monitoring, and a reversible path to human review.
- Gate expansion on task quality and privacy evidence, not signup volume alone.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use successful setup, task quality by relevant segment, false decisions, privacy exceptions, time to correction, retained use, and human escalation. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
